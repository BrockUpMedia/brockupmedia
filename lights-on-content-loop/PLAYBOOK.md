# Lights On 90-day social playbook (13 Oct 2026 to 15 Jan 2027)

The strategists and writers read this alongside STRATEGY.md. STRATEGY.md holds the facts and rules.
This file holds the plan: what each day's post is for.

## North star

**One signed brand partner by 15 January 2027.** Every post moves someone one step down this
path:

`Stop the scroll → Trust the proof → Start a conversation → Book a call with Liza`

Weekly numbers to watch, in this order:
1. Partner conversations started (DMs, comment keyword triggers, emails to brands@)
2. Partnerships page clicks
3. Saves and shares (the strongest signal that a post is good)
4. Reach and follower growth (useful, but never the goal)

## The big idea

Partnership deals are sold person to person, not page to page. Company pages on LinkedIn get
very little reach, while a founder's personal posts get several times more. So:

- **Liza's personal LinkedIn is the engine.** It gets 3 founder posts a week: her opinion, what
  she sees from the casting chair, the business case for inclusion. The Lights On Studio page
  reshares them and adds proof.
- **Instagram and Facebook build the public proof and the community.** Faces, training, results.
- **Every post serves one of four jobs:** proof, point of view, people, or partner offer.

## Weekly rhythm (Lights On)

| Day | Job | Instagram + Facebook | LinkedIn |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Proof | An outcome: booked work, a credit, the Screenrights backing | Studio page: the same proof, written for business readers |
| Tue | Point of view | Light quote card or Reel | **Liza personal:** founder post (market case, what casting directors actually need) |
| Wed | Behind the scenes | **Reel** from a training session (slate, marks, notes). Always captioned | Studio page: reshare Liza |
| Thu | Partner offer | Carousel: "What a brand gets when it partners with Lights On" | **Liza personal:** a short case or story ending in the partner ask |
| Fri | People | Graduate spotlight: headshot plus one line in their own words (with consent) | Studio page: spotlight |
| Sat | Community | Lighter post: a quote, a moment, a thank you | Skip |
| Sun | Recap and ask | "This week at Lights On" plus a clear CTA | **Liza personal:** weekly reflection, optional |

Priority of formats: Reels first (the most reach), then carousels (the most saves), then single
images. Every video carries burned-in captions, and every image has alt text. For a disability-led
brand, that's credibility as well as good practice.

## CTA ladder (rotate, one CTA per post)

- 3 of every 6 posts, soft: follow, save, share with a producer you know
- 2 of every 6, medium: **comment PARTNER**, which triggers an automatic DM with the partnerships
  page (Blotato DM automation, once Liza approves it)
- 1 of every 6, direct: partnerships.brockupmedia.com.au or brands@brockupmedia.com.au

## Three phases

**Phase 1: Expo sprint (13 to 31 October).** The Disability Expo is the first live lead
generator. Posts count down to the stand, and the goal is meetings booked at the expo, not likes.
The Expo audience is mostly people with disability, families and providers, so expect Cohort 3
applicants and service-provider partners as well as brands. Capture every contact.

**Phase 2: Proof and conversion (November).** Follow up with expo contacts within 48 hours. Post a
case-study carousel (three of five pilot participants booked paid work), the Screenrights backing,
Cohort 3 progress and spotlights on existing partners (indelarts, APAC, Inclusively Made, Creative
Brisbane Collab) so new brands see who's already in. Liza runs 3 founder posts a week.

**Phase 3: Close (December to mid January).** Brisbane 2032 framing ("inclusion can't wait until
the cameras are rolling"), year in review, and warm outreach: Liza picks 20 target brands from the
CSR Prospects sheet in Drive, engages with their posts for 2 weeks, then sends a direct message
pointing to the proof.

## Rules for every post

- The first line is the hook. If it wouldn't stop a producer scrolling, rewrite it.
- A face in the first frame wherever possible.
- One idea, one CTA.
- Never name a graduate without their consent being confirmed in STRATEGY.md.
- Every second week, check post analytics (`blotato_get_post_analytics` / `blotato_list_top_posts`).
  Do more of the top 20% format and topic, and drop what isn't working.

## Brock Casting (supporting role)

Three posts a week (Mon, Wed, Fri) on LinkedIn and Facebook, as set in
`brock-casting-content-loop/STRATEGY.md`. At least one post a week cross-promotes Lights On
through the casting director angle ("what I look for in the room"), which feeds partner trust.
