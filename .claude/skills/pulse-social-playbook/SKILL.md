# Pulse social playbook, applied to Liza's brands

Source: Pulse Social Media, "8 Steps to Look Stupidly Good on Social Media" (Liza, 2026-10-21).
This skill turns those 8 steps plus the bonus into rules and a weekly routine for every channel.

**Order of authority:** each brand's `STRATEGY.md` (locked facts, approval gate, Blotato IDs,
Liza's own rules) wins over this file. This file decides *how* posts are planned, hooked,
written, shot and followed up. Never publish without Liza's "go" in chat.

## Step 1: Dream customer (write for one person)

Every post is written to ONE of these people. Name them in the pack ("Written for: Dana").
These are working profiles drafted from the strategy files. Liza confirms or corrects them.

- **Lights On partner: Dana, 42, Head of Brand or Community Investment at a Queensland
  company** (confirmed by Liza, 2026-10-21). Has budget and
  a 2032 inclusion target, is scared of tokenism and of getting disability representation wrong
  in public. Scrolls LinkedIn at 7 to 8am and at lunch. Wants proof, safety and a simple way in.
- **Lights On community: Jess, 35, parent, carer or support worker of a young adult with
  disability** (confirmed by Liza, 2026-10-21). On Facebook and Instagram in the evening. Wants
  real pathways into screen work and to see people like her person on camera, treated as
  professionals. Just as important: she wants a community for her person, somewhere they build
  skills and friendships, find their tribe and grow in confidence while learning. Posts for Jess
  show belonging as much as training: friendships in the room, laughter, wins, confidence growing
  week by week.
- **Brock Casting client: Tom, 38, producer at a Brisbane agency or production company.** The
  brief landed today and the client wants options by Friday. Checks LinkedIn at midday. Wants a
  casting director with a ready network who makes the job easy.

Find better answers from real data, monthly: comments and DMs (their words become caption words),
follower insights (age, location, active hours via `blotato_get_post_analytics` and the native
apps), and who comments on peers' posts.

## Step 3: What to post (three buckets, 80/20)

Every post is tagged **Educate**, **Entertain** or **Sell** in the pack.

- Across each brand's week: about 80% Educate plus Entertain, at most 20% Sell. With 7 Lights On
  posts a week that means at most 1 or 2 Sell posts. Brock Casting at 3 a week: 1 Educate,
  1 Entertain, 1 Sell.
- Educate: how-tos, answers to questions producers and families actually ask, casting tips.
- Entertain: behind the scenes, bloopers, a graduate's moment, the slate, the notes session.
- Sell: the partner offer, book a casting, the Expo, an enquiry ask.
- Map onto Lights On's weekly rhythm in PLAYBOOK.md: Proof and People count as Educate or
  Entertain; Partner offer counts as Sell.

## Step 4: Hooks and captions (stop the scroll in 3 seconds)

For every post, write **three hook options**, pick the strongest and show the other two in the
pack so Liza can swap. A hook does one of these:

- Leads with the payoff: "How to cast authentically when the client wants options by Friday."
- Calls the person out: "If you run brand for a Queensland company, read this."
- Sparks curiosity: "Most brands get disability representation wrong in the first five seconds."

Never open with "We're excited", "Welcome", "Happy Monday" or the brand's name.
For video: open on the best shot, and put a bold text hook on screen in the first second
(with captions on, per the accessibility rule).

Caption rules: write like Liza talks (read it aloud), one idea per post, short lines with white
space, and end with exactly one call to action (Step 8).

## Step 5: Shooting on a phone (shot list in every pack)

The weekly pack includes a **shot list** of 3 to 5 clips or photos for next week, so the library
keeps growing with fresh, real content. Each item says what to shoot and how:

- Back camera, 60fps, vertical 9:16 for Reels, no zoom (1x or 0.5x).
- Light on the subject: face the window, never shoot with a window behind.
- Sound: quiet room, clip-on mic for anyone talking to camera.
- Steady: tripod or elbows tucked in. Leave headroom, subject off-centre, clean background, depth
  behind the subject.
- Showcase: move slow, mix wide and close, a few extra seconds at each end for trimming.
- Photos: take ten, keep one, candid beats posed, light edit only.
- Afterwards: auto-captions in CapCut or Descript (captions are mandatory, see accessibility).

Reels get the most reach, so aim for at least one Reel a week on Lights On Instagram once Liza
has footage. Until then, photos from Drive.

## Step 6: Batching (weeks of content in one afternoon)

- The Monday 6:52am pack writes the whole week at once. That is the batch.
- Suggest one 2-hour **content session** a week in the pack (day and time Liza picks) to shoot
  the shot list. Never put anything in Liza's calendar without her say-so.
- Done beats perfect: if a slot has no great idea, ship a simple honest post or leave the slot
  empty and say so. Never filler.

## Step 7: Consistency and engagement

- Keep the cadence each brand's STRATEGY.md sets (Lights On daily on every channel by Liza's
  choice, Brock Casting Mon, Wed, Fri). Note from the playbook: three solid posts a week beat ten
  then nothing. If quality slips, recommend fewer, better posts rather than filling slots.
- **Engagement is half the job.** Each Monday pack includes an **Engagement round**: pull the
  last week's comments with `blotato_list_comments` on published posts, and draft a reply for
  each one in the brand's voice. Replies are only posted after Liza approves them by number
  (`blotato_post_comment`). Flag any DM or comment that is a lead.
- Never run out of ideas: keep a running idea bank at the bottom of each pack (5 ideas, one line
  each, tagged by bucket).

## Step 8: Turn views into customers

- Every post has exactly one call to action, from the brand's STRATEGY.md:
  - Lights On: partnerships.brockupmedia.com.au, brands@brockupmedia.com.au, "comment PARTNER"
    once the DM automation is approved, or link in bio on Instagram.
  - Brock Casting: hello@brockcasting.com.au.
  - Expo posts: the registration link (see STRATEGY.md).
- Rotate soft (follow, save, share), medium (comment a keyword, DM) and direct (link, email).
- **Check every link in the pack works** before showing it (`curl -sI` for a 200 or 3xx). Flag
  any that fail.
- Every comment and DM that shows buying intent is a warm lead. List them in the pack with a
  suggested next step (reply, email, call). Get people off the platform: email or booking page.

## Bonus: Proof and trust

- Proof beats promises: before and afters, finished shoots, bookings, testimonials.
- Ask Liza each week for one piece of proof (a quote from a graduate, partner or client, a
  screenshot of a kind DM, a booking) and store it in a **Proof** folder in Drive. Never post a
  testimonial or name without consent.
- Show the human behind the business: Liza's face and voice on her personal LinkedIn every week.
- Remove the risk in Sell posts: "a free first chat", "no lock-in", whatever Liza confirms is true.

## The weekly pack checklist (run this every Monday)

For each post in the pack, show:

```
N. DAY CHANNEL, time Brisbane  |  Bucket: Educate/Entertain/Sell  |  Written for: Dana/Jess/Tom
Hook (chosen): ...
Alt hooks: ... / ...
[caption]
CTA: ...  (link checked)
Image N / alt text
```

Then, after the posts:

1. **Bucket balance**: count Educate, Entertain and Sell per brand. Fix it if Sell is over 20%.
2. **Engagement round**: drafted replies to last week's comments, plus any leads.
3. **Shot list** for next week's content session (3 to 5 items, Step 5 rules).
4. **Proof ask**: one specific thing to capture this week.
5. **Idea bank**: 5 one-line ideas tagged by bucket.
6. **Last week's numbers**: top post and why it worked, and one thing to do more of.

## Monthly (first Monday of the month)

Add a short review: top 20% of posts by saves, shares and comments (`blotato_list_top_posts`),
what format and hook they used, and one change for the month. Refresh the dream customer
profiles from what comments and DMs show.
