# Brock Casting content loop

Plans and writes client-facing Brock Casting posts three times a week, then schedules them in
Blotato once Liza approves them in chat. Same shape as `lights-on-content-loop/`.

1. `brock-casting-strategist` picks the day's angle.
2. `brock-casting-writer` drafts LinkedIn and Facebook, text only, in Liza's Brock Casting voice,
   every post ending with an email ask to hello@brockcasting.com.au.
3. `brock-casting-daily-digest` reports what's scheduled, published or failed on the Brock
   Casting pages.
4. `brock-casting-scheduler` runs only after Liza approves, and schedules with explicit times.

`STRATEGY.md` holds the goal, CTA, account ids, cadence, locked facts and voice rules.
`RUN.md` is the step-by-step for a post day.

Client, production and talent names are confidential by default. The loop never pulls from
the BROCK CASTING Drive folder (deal memos, quotes, headshots).
